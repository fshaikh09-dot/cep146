# Spicy Pasta

## Ingredients

- Pasta
- Tomato sauce
- Onion
- Garlic
- Chilli flakes
- Salt
- Cheese

## Cooking Steps

1. Boil the pasta until cooked.
2. Heat oil in a pan and add onion and garlic.
3. Add tomato sauce, chilli flakes, and salt.
4. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
5. Add cheese and serve hot.

**Cooking Tip:** Do not overcook the pasta.
