# Spicy Pasta

## Ingredients

- Pasta
- Tomato sauce
- Onion
- Garlic
- Chilli flakes
- Salt
- Cheese

## Cooking Steps

1. Boil the pasta until cooked.
2. Heat oil in a pan and add onion and garlic.
3. Add tomato sauce, chilli flakes, and salt.
4. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
5. Add cheese and serve hot.

**Cooking Tip:** Do not overcook the pasta.


# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose
To maintain a high standard of academic integrity across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning and working practices.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> Within an academic environment, academic integrity means a commitment to the fundamental values of **honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility and courage**. These values guide behaviour within academic communities.

### Cheating

**Cheating** involves obtaining or attempting to obtain credit or improved academic performance through dishonest or deceptive means.

### Contract Cheating

**Contract cheating** occurs when a student submits academic work created by a third party as their own work for academic credit.

### Plagiarism

**Plagiarism** involves using another person's or organization's work, ideas, words, images or other material and presenting it as one's own without properly citing the source.

### Unauthorized Aid

**Unauthorized aid** means using non-approved assistance to complete coursework or assessments, such as unauthorized collaboration, artificial intelligence technology, the internet or smartphones.

### Undeclared AI Use

**Undeclared AI use** means using artificial intelligence to generate part or all of an assessment without disclosing how and to what extent the technology was used.

## Policy

### 1. General
- All members of the Seneca community are expected to maintain **academic integrity**.
- Students are responsible for completing their academic work honestly and following Seneca's academic integrity requirements.
- Academic integrity violations may result in sanctions.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program
- Seneca maintains an **Academic Integrity Program** to promote academic integrity throughout the institution.
- The program provides education, resources and support related to academic integrity.
- It helps students and employees understand their responsibilities and the consequences of academic integrity violations.

### 3. Responsibilities
- **Students** are responsible for understanding and following academic integrity expectations.
- **Faculty members** are responsible for communicating academic integrity expectations and identifying possible violations.
- **Academic administrators** are responsible for ensuring academic integrity procedures are followed appropriately.
- Members of the Seneca community share responsibility for supporting a culture of academic integrity.

### 4. Sanctions
- Students who violate the Academic Integrity Policy may receive **sanctions**.
- Sanctions depend on the circumstances and severity of the academic integrity violation.
- Academic integrity violations and sanctions are handled according to Seneca's established procedures.

## Appendix A: Academic Integrity Procedure

### Information Gathering and Assessment

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Review a suspected academic integrity violation and gather relevant information. | As required | Faculty |
| Meet with the student to discuss the suspected violation and available information. | As required | Faculty and Student |
| Determine whether an academic integrity violation occurred and proceed according to the policy. | As required | Faculty / Academic Administrator |
